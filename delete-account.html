Delete your LoText account and data

Last updated: August 22, 2026

You can delete your LoText account at any time. There are two ways to do it - both are free of charge.

Option 1 - Delete your account in the app (instant)

  1. Open the LoText app on your device.
  2. Go to the Profile tab and open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm by typing DELETE.
  5. Your account is signed out immediately and the deletion is processed permanently on our servers.

Option 2 - Request deletion by email (no login required)

If you cannot sign in to the app, send a deletion request to:

officiallotext@gmail.com

Use the subject line "LoText account deletion request" and send it from the email address registered to your LoText account so we can verify you. We process verified requests within 7 days.

What data is deleted

  • Your profile: email address, username, display name, profile photo, and user ID
  • All your chat messages (text, photos, videos, voice messages) from all conversations
  • All your status updates
  • Your call history
  • Your contacts list and presence data (online / last seen)
  • Your push-notification device tokens
  • Your authentication credentials

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Residual copies that exist in encrypted automatic server backups are removed as those backups rotate, within 30 days at the latest.

What data may be kept

  • Messages you sent to other users are removed from your side; because conversations are shared, copies held for the other participants remain visible to them until they delete them.
  • We may retain minimal records where required by law (for example, to comply with legal obligations or defend against abuse).

Delete some data without deleting your account

You can remove individual items at any time without deleting your whole account: delete any message you have sent (long-press the message > Delete), and delete your status updates from the Status tab. For any other selective erasure requests, contact officiallotext@gmail.com.
