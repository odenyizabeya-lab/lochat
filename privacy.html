LoText Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

1. Overview

LoText ("we", "us") provides a messaging application ("the App") for mobile and other devices. This policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to the LoText app distributed on Google Play and to this website.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: email address, chosen username, display name, profile photo, and a unique user ID.
  • Content you create: chat messages (text, photos, videos, voice messages), status updates, and call history between LoText users.
  • Contacts: the LoText users you choose to add as contacts.
  • Device data: a push-notification device token used to deliver notifications.
  • Technical data: connection information needed to operate the service.

We do not require your phone number. We do not collect your precise location, contacts from your device address book, or advertising identifiers.

3. How we use your data

  • To provide messaging, calls, statuses, translation, and the AI assistant.
  • To deliver push notifications.
  • To keep your account and messages private and secure.
  • To provide support when you contact us.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. How we share data

We do not sell personal data. We do not share your messages with third parties. Messages are accessible only to the users in a conversation. Service providers (for example, cloud hosting) process data only to operate the App for you. We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.

5. AI assistant

The optional AI assistant sends the content you choose (for example, a message you ask to be rewritten or translated) to a third-party AI provider in order to generate the result. Your content is not used to train those providers' models. Please do not send sensitive information through the AI assistant that you do not wish to share.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • You can delete individual messages and status updates in the App.
  • You can delete your entire account in the App (Profile > account deletion). Deleting your account removes your profile, messages, statuses, call history, and associated data.
  • You may also request deletion of your data by contacting us at officiallotext@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored with per-user access rules so that only you and the people you communicate with can access your content. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, but we work hard to protect your information.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
