Real-time messaging

Text, photos, videos, and voice messages with read receipts, typing indicators, and replies.

Voice & video calls

Crystal-clear one-on-one voice and video calls between LoText users.

Statuses

Voice changer

Voice messages with a fun built-in voice-changer to keep conversations playful.

Automatic translation

Incoming messages are translated into your language automatically, so anyone can chat.

Built-in AI assistant

Draft replies, rewrite messages, summarize chats, and translate with the optional AI assistant.

Find friends easily

Add people by username or a unique LoText ID. No phone number required to sign up.

Your conversations are protected with per-user server-side access rules. Only the people you message can read your messages.
