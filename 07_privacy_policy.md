# LoText - Privacy Policy Requirements

Google Play requires a **public, HTTPS privacy policy URL** for apps that collect
user data (LoText does). You must host this page before the app can be reviewed.

## Requirements

1. A live HTTPS URL (no "under construction").
2. Clearly describe what data LoText collects, how it's used, and how users can
   control/delete it.
3. Must match the Data Safety declarations (`02_data_safety.md`).

## Recommended hosting (pick one)

- A **GitHub Pages** site (`https://<user>.github.io/lotext/privacy`)
- A free **Notion / Google Sites / Wix** public page
- Your own domain (if you have one)

## Privacy Policy template

Copy, paste, replace the bracketed `[ ]` parts, and publish.

---

# LoText Privacy Policy

*Last updated: [DATE]*

## 1. Overview
LoText ("we", "us") provides a messaging application ("the App"). This policy
explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

## 2. Data we collect
- **Account data:** email address, chosen username, display name, profile photo,
  and a unique user ID.
- **Content you create:** chat messages (text, photos, videos, voice messages),
  status updates, and call history between LoText users.
- **Contacts:** the LoText users you choose to add as contacts.
- **Device data:** a push-notification device token to deliver notifications.
- **Technical data:** connection information needed to operate the service.

## 3. How we use your data
- To provide messaging, calls, statuses, and the AI assistant.
- To deliver notifications.
- To keep your account and messages private and secure.

## 4. How we share data
We do not sell personal data. We do not share your messages with third parties.
Messages are accessible only to the users in a conversation. Service providers
(e.g. cloud hosting) process data only to operate the App for you.

## 5. AI assistant
The optional AI assistant sends the content you choose (e.g. a message you ask
to be rewritten) to a third-party AI provider to generate the result. Content is
not used to train those providers. Do not send sensitive information you do not
wish to share.

## 6. Data retention and deletion
- You can delete individual messages and statuses in the App.
- You can delete your entire account in the App (profile > account deletion);
  this removes your profile, messages, and associated data.
- To request removal, contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL].

## 7. Security
Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored with per-user access rules. No
transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

## 8. Children
The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect
data from them.

## 9. Changes
We may update this policy; material changes will be posted here.

## 10. Contact
Questions: [SUPPORT EMAIL].
